Our Instructors

Meet the Staff

The Team

Experienced Instructors,
Dedicated to Your Growth

Each member of our team brings professional performance experience and a passion for teaching. Get to know the people who will guide your musical journey.

Marcus Webb

Piano Instructor

Marcus holds a degree in Music Performance from Berklee College of Music and has spent over a decade performing jazz and gospel across the Southeast. He specializes in helping beginners build a strong theoretical foundation while keeping lessons engaging and fun. His patience and clear communication style make him a favorite among students of all ages.

Elena Vasquez

Guitar Instructor

Elena is a classically trained guitarist with a deep love for Latin, flamenco, and fingerstyle techniques. She studied at the Conservatorio Superior de Música in Madrid before relocating to the United States to pursue teaching. Elena works with students on acoustic and electric guitar and places a strong emphasis on ear training and musical expression.

Naomi Clarke

Bass Instructor

Naomi has been playing bass since she was thirteen, rooted in the gospel and funk traditions of her upbringing. She has toured regionally with several R&B acts and now brings that real-world experience directly into her teaching. Her lessons focus on groove, rhythm, and developing the kind of pocket feel that makes a band sound tight from the very start.

James "JD" Dixon

Drums Instructor

JD is a high-energy drummer with a background in rock, R&B, and marching percussion. He has performed with multiple local bands and spent three years as a drum line instructor at the collegiate level. JD tailors every lesson to the student's musical interests, making sure that building technique never comes at the cost of excitement and creative expression.
