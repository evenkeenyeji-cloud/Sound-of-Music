About Us

Who We Are

A School Built on
Passion for Music

Sound of Music was founded with a single belief: that anyone — regardless of age or background can learn to play an instrument with the right guidance. We created a space where aspiring musicians feel welcome, challenged, and inspired from their very first lesson.

Our instructors bring professional performance experience alongside a genuine love for teaching. Every student who walks through our doors is met with a personalized approach, because no two musical journeys look the same.

"If art is how we fill space, Music is how we fill time"

Whether you are picking up an instrument for the first time or looking to sharpen skills you already have, Sound of Music offers structured programs in piano, guitar, bass, and drums to meet you exactly where you are.
